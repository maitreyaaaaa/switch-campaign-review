# Demo video storyboard

**Target:** 60 seconds, 1920x1080, 16:9 at 30 fps.
**Story:** risky fictional claim -> shared Switch room -> evidence narrows the wording -> person chooses.
**Visual style:** coded Switch Console UI recreation with fictional campaign evidence. Keep the illustrative-demo and fictional-data labels visible.
**Sound:** locally generated Kokoro narration and a quiet, licensed Mixkit instrumental bed; no artificial UI sounds.
**Captions:** synchronized and burned in, plus a separate SRT.

| Time | Picture and motion | Spoken point | Read/pacing job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:03 | Hook card over labeled UI recreation: Will this claim hold up? | Check what the evidence supports. | Hook on frame one. |
| 0:03-0:13.5 | Recreated teammate draft. Highlight every account, setup in seconds, and 80 percent. | Draft and reviewer request enter the room. | One claim at a time. |
| 0:13.5-0:22 | Explanatory rail: teammate -> Switch room -> reviewer -> evidence. | Show the person-and-agent workflow. | Rail is an overlay, not product UI. |
| 0:22-0:34.5 | Fictional campaign results: supported, contradicted, unknown, unknown. | State each finding and scope. | Hold each row while read. |
| 0:34.5-0:42.2 | Match supported row to narrower suggested wording. | The wording stays within the evidence. | Keep focus on the claim. |
| 0:42.2-0:51.9 | Recreated USE SUGGESTION choice and reviewer echo showing `DECISION_RECORDED`. | The reviewer confirms the person's choice in the room. | No campaign edit or publication is implied. |
| 0:51.9-1:00 | Labeled close: Evidence first. People decide. | Review claims together before publication. | Music tails under the close. |

## Capture and edit rules
- Do not present the coded interface as a screen recording.
- Keep the UI-recreation and fictional-data labels visible throughout.
- The reviewer echo was internally verified. Do not imply a campaign write or publication.
- Use direct cuts and one match cut from supported finding to suggested wording.
- Avoid blank transitions and keep UI text readable in the full-size export.
