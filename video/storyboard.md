# Demo video storyboard

**Target:** 75–90 seconds, 1920×1080, 16:9  
**Style:** real product capture with a restrained Official-inspired skin: `#fffefa` ground, `#20342e` ink, `#31583d` green, and `#b49c67` gold accent; DM Sans and Georgia. Keep all overlay text at least 28 px at 1080p. No generated product screens or unrelated feature tour.  
**Audio:** user's recorded narration, no music by default  
**Captions:** synchronized, burned in, plus `.srt`

## Capture rules

- Record the actual fixture review in the private fallback room; do not delete or alter the existing reference channel.
- Show the actual messages and setup. Do not insert a fabricated verdict, evidence panel, chat response, or human decision.
- Keep browser address bars, passwords, account email, local credentials, and unrelated tabs out of frame.
- Keep `Unknown` results visible. The live Official source is unavailable, so label all evidence as fictional local demo data.
- Keep the decision at `WAITING_FOR_HUMAN_CHOICE`; no human choice has been recorded for this rehearsal.
- Review the final export at full size for caption timing, legibility, crop, and truthful representation before publishing.
