# SCRIPT - Campaign Review Room

**Target:** 60 seconds at 1920x1080 and 30 fps.
**Voice:** synthetic Kokoro f_sky, 58.78 seconds, speed 1.0.
**Captions:** local Whisper word timing, corrected against the script; burned in and supplied as SRT.
**Evidence:** explicitly fictional local campaign data. The coded UI is an illustrative recreation, not a screen recording.

## Scene plan

| Time | Beat | Picture | Spoken point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3s | Hook | Labeled Switch UI recreation with the question,  Will this claim hold up? | Before a claim goes live, check what the evidence supports. |
| 3-13.5s | Risky draft | Show the fictional request; highlight every account, seconds, and 80 percent. | The draft enters a Switch room for review. |
| 13.5-22s | Shared workflow | Reveal teammate, existing reviewer, and evidence path. | Switch joins the teammate and reviewer; the adapter checks claims against evidence. |
| 22-34.5s | Findings | Show supported, contradicted, and unknown rows. | State the scope and each result. |
| 34.5-42.2s | Safer wording | Match the supported audience to the narrower suggested line and concise-copy check. | The suggestion stays within the evidence. |
| 42.2-51.9s | Human choice | Show USE SUGGESTION and the reviewer echo with `DECISION_RECORDED`. | The choice is recorded in the Switch room; no campaign write or publication is implied. |
| 51.9-60s | Close | Hold the labeled room, then reveal Evidence first. People decide. | Teams can review claims together before publication. |

## Guardrails
- Keep the fictional-data and illustrative-demo labels visible in every scene.
- Do not imply a real reviewer echo, durable decision, campaign edit, or publication.
- Keep UI text readable at 1080p; use close crops and restrained highlights to direct attention.
- Keep a meaningful visual change every 2-5 seconds. Avoid blank holds and unnecessary acronym explanations.
