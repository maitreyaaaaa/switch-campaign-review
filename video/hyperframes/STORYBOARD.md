## Frame 1 - The question (0-3s)
- Show the coded Switch UI with the illustrative-demo and fictional-data labels visible.
- Land the hook: Will this claim hold up?
- Voice: Before a claim goes live, check what the evidence supports.

## Frame 2 - Risky draft enters the room (3-13.5s)
- Show the fictional CSV export draft and highlight each risky claim in order.
- Keep the claim clearly framed as draft language, not fact.
- Voice: This fictional draft promises CSV export to every account, setup in seconds, and 80 percent fewer manual errors. A teammate asks the reviewer to check it in a Switch room.

## Frame 3 - Shared workflow (13.5-22s)
- Build a small workflow rail: teammate -> Switch room -> reviewer -> evidence.
- Label the rail as an explanatory overlay; it is not product UI.
- Voice: Switch brings the teammate and an existing reviewer into one shared conversation. A read-only adapter checks each claim against the campaign evidence.

## Frame 4 - Evidence narrows the claim (22-34.5s)
- Show fictional results: CSV export for internal testers in the US is supported; every-account access is contradicted; setup time and 80 percent reduction are unknown.
- Highlight each result as it is spoken, then hold the full set.
- Voice: This review uses fictional campaign data. CSV export is supported for internal testers in the US today. Access for every account is contradicted. Setup time and the 80 percent result are unknown.

## Frame 5 - Safer wording (34.5-42.2s)
- Match the supported result to the narrower suggested sentence.
- Keep the short-copy check subordinate to the evidence result. No ASD-STE100 reference.
- Voice: The reviewer suggests a narrower line: Internal testers in the US can use CSV export today. It stays within the evidence.

## Frame 6 - Human choice (42.2-51.9s)
- Show USE SUGGESTION and the reviewer echo with `DECISION_RECORDED`.
- The reviewer echo was internally verified; no campaign content changed or was published.
- Voice: A person chooses USE SUGGESTION with the review reference. The reviewer echoes that choice in the room, and the workflow records DECISION_RECORDED. Nothing was changed or published.

## Frame 7 - Evidence first (51.9-60s)
- Return to the labeled room recreation; reveal Evidence first. People decide. as the spoken close lands.
- Voice: With verified rollout evidence, teams can review claims together before publication. Evidence first. People decide.

## Pacing and sound
- 60 seconds with continuous synthetic narration, timed captions, and a quiet licensed Mixkit instrumental bed.
- Make a meaningful visual change every 2-5 seconds; keep each evidence result stable while it is read.
- No black slates, blank gaps, fake cursor, fabricated product sounds, or undocumented live-product state.
- All UI is recreated and all campaign results are fictional data from the connected Official demo endpoint.
